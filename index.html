Questions shop owners ask

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. StockFlow opens in your phone browser, so there is nothing to install. It works on computers and tablets too.

Is my business data safe?

Your data is stored in a secure database, and each business can only see its own records. StockFlow does not keep your business data in your browser.

Can I record M-Pesa sales?

Yes. When you record a sale you can choose Cash, M-Pesa, Bank or Credit as the payment method.

How much does it cost?

It is free during early access. When paid plans start, you will see the price and can choose a plan or stay on the free one.

Do I need internet?

Yes. StockFlow needs an internet connection to save and load your data.
